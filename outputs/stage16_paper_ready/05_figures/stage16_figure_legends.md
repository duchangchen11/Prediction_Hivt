# Stage16 figure legends

These original code schematics and empirical plots use the nuScenes adaptation in this repository. Empirical evaluation is custom scene-isolated three-fold CV on 630 official TRAIN scenes, with 260,151 full-horizon actor windows. It is not the official nuScenes prediction test. Each fold has a separately fitted Stage5A predictor; ranking methods share its fixed six candidates. Actor windows within a scene are dependent. Main figures retain the original Stage15B checkpoints and scores. The source-data CSVs and figure/source SHA256 are listed in `stage16_figure_manifest.json`; local nuScenes case coordinates require access to the original dataset and frozen cache.

**Fig. 1 | Fixed candidate generation and regret-aware ranking.** The code schematic shows the adapted HiVT-64 backbone with type embedding, the Stage5A motion-conditioned residual decoder, six candidate trajectories, and the Future Interaction Graph scoring head. The original pi head uses the original HiVT embeddings; the graph receives observable candidate features and original logits/probabilities, not latent HiVT embeddings. Green boxes denote graph computation; blue boxes denote fixed candidates and selection; tan boxes denote head-training labels and objective. Detached future endpoint errors enter Loss C and development evaluation only. They are absent from inference features. G-C adds a learned score residual to the original logits and preserves candidate geometry. Bicycle inference uses the same-fold frozen R2 route. Parameter counts are 650,403 for the predictor, 24,066 for G1, and 673 for routed R2. This is a structural diagram; there is no empirical sample or error bar.

**Fig. 2 | Candidate-conditioned future interaction graph.** Six mode nodes of the target and six mode nodes of one illustrative neighbor show the 36 candidate-pair relations. Each node has 15 features and each relation has 17 features. Actual neighborhoods contain at most eight actors within 50 m at the observation endpoint. For each target mode, masked attention aggregates up to 48 neighbor modes. Message inputs have 47 features, attention inputs have 145 features, and the aggregated message has 64 channels. Relations summarize predicted geometry, original probabilities and participant types. Candidate trajectories, rather than future GT, define these relations. The drawing illustrates the implemented directed relation calculation; it is not a sampled physical scene or joint multi-agent mode predictor.

**Fig. 3 | Seven-model performance on shared candidates.** The panels show Top1FDE and Top1ADE in meters; smaller values indicate lower selected-trajectory error. Filled dots show actor-window-weighted pooled means; short ticks show the three fold-specific means, not confidence intervals or seed replicates. R0 is the adapted Stage5A generator's original scoring head, not an unmodified published HiVT benchmark result. R2 is the original reliability ranker. NG/G indicate absent/present post-prediction graph messages; A/C indicate soft-target cross-entropy/normalized expected regret. Matched-NG-C uses a target-only capacity control. All models share oracle minFDE6 of 1.266202 m. The denominator is 260,151 actor windows from 630 scenes; each fold has 210 OuterTest scenes. Source data: `stage16_seven_model_metrics.csv`.

**Fig. 4 | Graph and loss ablation.** Panel a shows pooled Top1FDE in meters for the graph-by-loss two-by-two design. Panel b shows the original three co-primary paired contrasts: G-C minus NG-C, G-C minus Matched-NG-C, and NG-C minus NG-A. Negative differences favor the first model. Intervals are the frozen Stage15B Bonferroni-adjusted 98.333% percentile intervals for a family of three comparisons, using 2,000 paired scene-cluster bootstrap draws with seed2022. Scenes are resampled within each of the three folds; models share each resample. Intervals are conditional on the fitted predictors and heads and do not incorporate predictor-training variability. The denominator is 260,151 actor windows from 630 scenes. Source data: `stage16_seven_model_metrics.csv` and `stage16_primary_bootstrap.csv`.

**Fig. 5 | Participant-group performance.** Panels show Top1FDE in meters for Vehicle (191,026 actor windows, 629 scenes), Pedestrian (66,145, 542), Bicycle (2,980, 169), and MovingVehicle (41,728, 597). Each panel has its own explicitly labeled scale. Points show pooled means; connecting lines aid reading and do not represent a continuous model parameter. MovingVehicle uses the frozen observation-time `vehicle.moving` attribute, not a newly optimized GT motion threshold. Bicycle uses the same-fold R2 route for all learned comparison heads; its identical scores do not establish a graph benefit. Group comparisons are descriptive secondary analyses. Source data: `stage16_seven_model_metrics.csv`.

**Fig. 6 | Selected improvement and failure cases.** Four panels show registered extrema of G-C minus NG-C Top1FDE: one improvement and one failure each for MovingVehicle and Pedestrian. They are selected from the 260,151-window, 630-scene pool and illustrate possible behavior, not prevalence or a random sample. Gray curves show the unchanged six candidates, black curves show future GT, dotted curves show observed history, and colored dashed/solid curves show the NG-C/G-C selected candidate. NG-C uses square endpoint markers and G-C uses circles. Coordinates are in the frozen observation-endpoint ego frame, in meters; all trajectories within each panel share the same axis and equal spatial scale. Different panels may use different extents. Coordinates and timestamps come directly from local frozen contexts, with endpoint-error and identity checks; no trajectories are reconstructed from aggregate FDE. Source data and original context SHA256: `07_cases/stage16_case_manifest.json`. Coordinate CSVs remain local.

**Fig. S1 | Oracle-mode score quality.** Panel a shows fixed-bin reliability for NG-A, NG-C, G-A and G-C. Confidence is the selected mode's softmax score; the event is that its index equals the fixed candidate set's minimum endpoint-FDE mode, with lowest-index exact tie breaking. Fifteen equal-width bins were registered; only nonempty bins are shown. The diagonal denotes agreement between confidence and event frequency. Panel b shows the unnormalized categorical six-mode Brier score, mean sum of squared differences from the one-hot oracle label (range 0–2; smaller is better). These scores do not evaluate trajectory coverage, a natural maneuver class, or calibrated future density. No calibration parameters are fitted. The denominator is 260,151 actor windows from 630 scenes. Source data: `stage16_probability_metrics.csv` and `stage16_reliability_bins.csv`.

**Fig. S2 | Conditional ranking-head initialization sensitivity.** Panels show three fold-specific and one pooled Overall Top1FDE comparisons in meters for NG-A, NG-C, G-C and Matched-NG-C. An open circle marks the original initialization; a triangle and square mark the registered +1000 and +2000 initialization seeds. Short horizontal lines show means across the three initializations, without an uncertainty interval. Each fold retains its predictor, candidate geometry, fitted normalization and batch order. New head checkpoints are selected only on InnerDev and all 24 freeze before new Outer scores are evaluated. Fold denominators are 82,673, 83,711 and 93,767 actor windows, each from 210 scenes. The pooled denominator is 260,151 windows from 630 scenes. Pooled configurations align the registered replicate indices across folds; they are not independently trained predictors. The supplement measures head initialization sensitivity only. Source data: `stage16_seed_metrics.csv` and `stage16_seed_summary.csv`.
